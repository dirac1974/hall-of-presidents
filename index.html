How to use the Hall

One loop. Keep it short.
  1. Place a portrait. Look at the silly picture. Say “#1 Washington” out loud.
  2. Walk the doors. The next door in order. Name who lives there.
  3. Line them up once you have four. Tap 1-2-3-4 in real order.
  4. Test when a few doors feel familiar. Type or say the name. No multiple choice.
  5. When it shines, the next new portrait is ready. Repeat.

The big green button on the Hall is always the best next step.
